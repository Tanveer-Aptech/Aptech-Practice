Our story

Made for coffee lovers who slow down and savor.

Velvet Roast began with a simple idea: coffee should feel like comfort, craft, and connection all at once. We built a welcoming café where rich aromas, thoughtful service, and beautifully brewed drinks turn ordinary moments into rituals.

Whether you're meeting a friend, reading a book, or starting your day, we create a space that feels warm, elevated, and easy to return to.
